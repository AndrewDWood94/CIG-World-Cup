* Highest Scoring Game: The £60 is split equally between the owners of all teams involved in the joint-highest scoring game(s). For example, if the highest total is 8 goals, and there are two games with 8 goals (e.g. 8–0 and 4–4), all four team owners share the £60.

** Fewest Points: If multiple teams finish the group stage on the same lowest points total, the prize goes to the team with the worst goal difference. If still tied, it is split equally between those teams.
